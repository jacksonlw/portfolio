# Portfolio

Jackson Lawrence's personal site: a single page drawn as a constellation of who he is, what he built and the skills behind it.

## Language

### The page

**Constellation**:
The whole page: Jackson at its center, his work and projects around him, and his skills grouped at the edges.
_Avoid_: Graph, board, network, skills map

**Me node**:
Jackson's own body at the center of the constellation: his photo, name and a one-sentence bio that is always visible.
_Avoid_: Profile card, hero, center node

**Satellite**:
A small body orbiting the Me node that takes the visitor somewhere: the resume, email, GitHub or a press piece.
_Avoid_: Link node, CTA, contact button

### Work and skills

**Bubble**:
A job or a project, shown as a disc sized by how much it matters. Every bubble is implicitly Jackson's, so none is tied to the Me node.
_Avoid_: Project node, card, tile

**Star**:
One skill, placed in its area's cluster and tied to the bubbles where it was used.
_Avoid_: Skill node, tag, chip

**Area**:
A named group of related stars, drawn as one cluster around a text label.
_Avoid_: Category, hub, skill group

**Illustration**:
The small cut-paper drawing inside every bubble's and satellite's disc, showing plainly what it stands for (a resume, a database). Never a logo, never the name set in type.
_Avoid_: Icon, badge, mark, logo

**Style**:
How every illustration is drawn: the pen's wobble, line weight, fills and colour. One style applies to all illustrations at once.
_Avoid_: Theme, look
